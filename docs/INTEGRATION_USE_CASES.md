# Bank Integration Use Cases for MechanicOnDemand

## Quick Reference Guide

This document provides practical use cases for integrating MechanicOnDemand with Malaysian banks (Ryt Bank, RHB Bank, and others).

---

## 🎯 Primary Use Cases

### 1. **Payment Processing**
**Problem**: Customers need convenient, secure payment options
**Solution**: Integrate multiple payment methods from partner banks
**Impact**: 
- Faster checkout (reduced cart abandonment)
- Lower transaction fees (0.5-1% vs 2-3%)
- Better user experience

**Implementation**:
- Ryt Wallet (AI-powered)
- RHB PayNow
- Bank transfers
- Credit/debit cards

---

### 2. **Instant Financing for Repairs**
**Problem**: Customers can't afford expensive repairs upfront
**Solution**: Offer 0% interest installment plans through bank partnerships
**Impact**:
- Increased average transaction value
- Higher customer satisfaction
- New revenue stream for banks

**Example**:
- Customer needs RM 1,500 repair
- Apply for 6-month installment plan
- Instant approval via AI credit scoring
- Pay RM 250/month with 0% interest

---

### 3. **AI-Powered Customer Support**
**Problem**: High support costs and slow response times
**Solution**: Integrate Ryt AI for intelligent customer service
**Impact**:
- 24/7 support availability
- 70% reduction in support costs
- Faster issue resolution

**Example**:
- Customer: "My car won't start"
- Ryt AI: Analyzes symptoms, suggests possible issues, recommends mechanics, provides price estimates
- Customer books mechanic in 2 minutes

---

### 4. **Business Financing for Mechanics**
**Problem**: Mechanics need capital for tools and equipment
**Solution**: Quick loan applications through bank integration
**Impact**:
- Mechanics can grow their business
- Better service quality
- Increased platform supply

**Example**:
- Mechanic needs RM 5,000 for diagnostic equipment
- Apply through app
- AI credit assessment (using transaction history)
- Approval in 24 hours
- Equipment delivered in 3 days

---

### 5. **Insurance Integration**
**Problem**: Customers want protection against unexpected repairs
**Solution**: On-demand repair insurance through bank partnerships
**Impact**:
- Predictable costs for customers
- New revenue stream
- Increased customer retention

**Example**:
- Customer subscribes to RM 50/month repair insurance
- Covers up to RM 500 per repair
- Unlimited claims per month
- Direct billing to insurance

---

### 6. **Loyalty & Rewards**
**Problem**: Need to increase customer retention
**Solution**: Bank rewards points integration
**Impact**:
- Higher transaction frequency
- Increased customer lifetime value
- Cross-selling opportunities

**Example**:
- Customer earns 100 points per RM 100 spent
- 1,000 points = RM 10 discount
- Exclusive offers for bank cardholders
- Points redeemable for future repairs

---

### 7. **Predictive Maintenance**
**Problem**: Customers don't plan for maintenance costs
**Solution**: AI predicts future maintenance needs and costs
**Impact**:
- Better financial planning
- Preventive maintenance
- Reduced breakdowns

**Example**:
- AI analyzes car age, mileage, usage
- Predicts: "Your brake pads need replacement in 3 months (RM 300)"
- Customer can save RM 25/month
- Automatic booking reminder

---

### 8. **Workshop Business Banking**
**Problem**: Workshops struggle with cash flow management
**Solution**: Integrated business banking solutions
**Impact**:
- Better financial management
- Automated payroll
- Inventory financing

**Example**:
- Workshop receives payments directly to business account
- Automated payroll for 5 mechanics
- Inventory financing for spare parts
- Real-time financial dashboard

## 🔄 Integration Flow Examples

### Example 1: Customer Payment Flow
```
1. Customer completes service
2. Receives invoice (RM 500)
3. Selects "Ryt Wallet" payment
4. Ryt AI suggests: "Use installment? Save 10%"
5. Customer chooses 3-month plan
6. Instant approval via Provenir
7. Payment processed
8. Mechanic receives payment (T+1)
9. Customer earns 50 reward points
```

### Example 2: Mechanic Financing Flow
```
1. Mechanic needs equipment (RM 10,000)
2. Applies through app
3. AI analyzes:
   - Transaction history
   - Customer ratings
   - Earnings pattern
4. Credit decision: Approved (24 hours)
5. Equipment ordered
6. Loan disbursed
7. Repayment: 10% of earnings
8. Auto-deducted monthly
```

### Example 3: Insurance Claim Flow
```
1. Customer has repair insurance
2. Car breaks down
3. Books mechanic through app
4. Repair completed (RM 300)
5. Invoice submitted
6. AI processes claim
7. Insurance approves (instant)
8. Customer pays RM 0 (covered)
9. Insurance pays mechanic
```
